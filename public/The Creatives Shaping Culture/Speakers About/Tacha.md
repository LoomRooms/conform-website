Anita Natacha Akide, popularly known as Tacha, is a Nigerian media personality, entrepreneur, and reality TV star who rose to fame as a housemate on Big Brother Naija Season 4 (2019). As of March 2026, she is a multi-title Guinness World Record holder for her achievements in the beauty industry.

Current Status and Recent Achievements
Tacha has recently solidified her status as a global record-breaker and philanthropist: 
Guinness World Records: In late 2025, Tacha officially secured two world records:
Most cosmetic makeovers in 8 hours: 82 makeovers.
Most cosmetic makeovers in 24 hours: 144 (or 145) makeovers.
She received her official plaques in March 2026.
Philanthropy & Public Health: In March 2026, she collaborated with the Lagos State Government to provide free eye care and health checks for police officers in Lagos.
Politics & Business: Tacha has publicly hinted at entering politics to support development in her home state, Rivers State. She also recently launched Tacha FC, a football club venture.
