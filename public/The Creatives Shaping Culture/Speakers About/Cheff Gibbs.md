GBOLABO GIBBS ADEBAKIN
Chef | Hospitality Consultant | Culinary Strategist | Restaurateur | Curator
Chef Gbolabo “Chef Gibbs” Adebakin is a visionary culinary director and hospitality expert
whose work continues to place Nigerian cuisine firmly on the global map. From his earliest
days stirring jollof in his mother’s kitchen to orchestrating world-class dining experiences, Chef
Gibbs has built a career defined by innovation, excellence, and a deep belief in the power of
food to unite cultures and drive economic growth.
His strategic leadership has been proven on some of Nigeria’s biggest culinary stages. He
served as Culinary Director for Hilda Baci’s Guinness World Record 100-hour cooking
marathon, and also led as Culinary Director for the Guinness World Record of the largest
serving of Nigerian-style jollof rice—another landmark achievement that spotlighted Nigerian
flavors while showcasing his operational precision at scale. He has further shaped major food
experiences as Culinary Director for leading festivals, including the Lagos Food Festival and
the Pepper Soup Festival, curating memorable culinary programming, coordinating complex
production teams, and delivering guest experiences that match international standards. Chef
Gibbs sits on the success of so many prominent brands and names as Producer and Director.
As the mastermind behind the Bistro at the Theatre—a pioneering fusion of fine dining and
performing arts. Chef Gibbs continually redefines how and where we experience exceptional
food. Beyond the kitchen, he leads the African Concierge Network as CEO and Founder,
consulting for luxury hospitality brands across the continent. His tenure as the former Vice
President of the Culinary Arts Practitioners Association of Nigeria and his current role as
Director of Events and Partnerships for the Restaurant, Café, Bar & Clubs Association (RCBC)
have galvanized the food industry, championing standards of excellence and forging
partnerships that elevate the entire industry. As Convener of the Nigeria Food Summit, he
brings together food professionals to strengthen the nation’s value chain and spotlight Nigerian
talent on the world stage.
Chef Gibbs’s impact extends to philanthropy through his NGO, where he fights childhood
hunger and empowers women with life-changing opportunities. A household name after
winning Mastercard’s “The Kitchen Nigeria,” he continues to mentor the next generation of
chefs, reduce food waste, and boost operational performance—having driven a 30% revenue
increase and 20% cost reduction at one of Nigeria’s top restaurants. With over thirteen years of
culinary and hospitality excellence, Chef Gibbs remains a storyteller and champion of Nigerian
gastronomy, crafting experiences that celebrate heritage, inspire innovation, and leave an
indelible mark on every table he touches.
