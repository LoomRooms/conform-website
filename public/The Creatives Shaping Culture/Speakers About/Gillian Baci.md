Gillian Bassey, widely known as Gillian Baci, is a multi-talented Nigerian pop artist, author, and filmmaker. Born Gilbert Bassey, he is also the brother of famous Nigerian chef and Guinness World Record holder, Hilda Baci. 
Music and Artistry
Baci describes his work as "philosophical pop music". He officially launched his music career in 2016 and is known for creating soul-stirring melodies with deep, emotional lyrics. 
Key Projects: His discography includes the EP Sweet Serenity and singles such as "Feel at Home," "Fired Up," and "Shege".
Film & Writing: A graduate of the MultiChoice Talent Factory, he has directed short films and written over 10 original songs for film and TV. 
Literacy and Authorship
In addition to music, he is an accomplished novelist. 
Published Work: He is the author of the novel A Decent Man.
Initiatives: He leads a story-teaching initiative called Learn Storycraft to mentor aspiring writers.
