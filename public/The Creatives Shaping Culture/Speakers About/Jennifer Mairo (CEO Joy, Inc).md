Official Profile
Jennifer Mairo is a multi-sector professional and leader with nearly two decades of experience spanning project management, media, film, and organizational transformation.

She currently serves as the Chief Executive Officer of Joy, Inc. Under her leadership, Joy, Inc has collaborated with organizations such as The Gates Foundation, National Endowment for Democracy, Africa No Filter, The Creator Economy Practice amongst others.

Before Joy, Inc., Jennifer worked as Principal and Program Director at RAS Studios, where she partnered with Dallas Film School and other institutions across the United States to mentor and train emerging filmmakers. Alongside others, she designed an educational film module implemented across 20 U.S. states and has graduated over 100 students under the program.

As a filmmaker and producer, her body of work includes; #WithChude, Kanaani, Issues of the Heart, She Stole Our Ovaries, Your Kidney or Your Life, The TikTok Scammer, among others. She has also produced international film festivals, including the African Film Festival (TAFF) and PressPlay Film Festival.

Jennifer also heads the Fourthmainland Creator Fund, a pioneering initiative and the first of its kind out of Nigeria, dedicated to supporting young African creatives and storytellers across the continent.

She holds a B.A. in French Language and Literature, a B.Sc. in Legal Studies and an M.Sc. in Public Administration and Policy from Pennsylvania State University (Penn State) plus certifications in Organizational Development and Human Resource Management.

She is a two time published author, “Miss Independent, Misunderstood” and “Screw it, Go Ahead and Quit Cold Turkey”.

She is known for her ability to build focused, healthy, and high-performing teams.
