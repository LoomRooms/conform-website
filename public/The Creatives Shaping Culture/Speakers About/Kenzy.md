Kenzy Udosen (born Ekene Mfoniso Nna-Udosen) is a Nigerian content creator, media personality, and actor best known for creating the viral comedic character Madam Theresa Omemgbojiso. He is recognized for his cultural commentary and relatable social reflections, which earned him a nomination at the Africa Magic Viewers' Choice Awards (AMVCA).

Professional Profile
Content Creation: He gained widespread fame through his "Madam Theresa" persona, a character known for her wit, signature red lipstick, and the Ndumodu Series.
Media & Hosting: Udosen is a dynamic live event host and red carpet presenter. On March 15, 2026, he served as an official host for the Moment Creator Awards Night at the Landmark Event Centre in Lagos.
Acting: He has transitioned into Nollywood, appearing in films such as Smokescreen (2023), All's Fair in Love (2024), and The Judge (2022)
