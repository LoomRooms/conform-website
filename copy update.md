CON/FORM 2026 — GENERAL OVERVIEW

What CON/FORM Is

CON/FORM is a one-day gathering designed to create meaningful dialogue between generations of Nigeria’s creative industry.

It brings together the people who built the systems that shaped the industry and the people who are now building their careers inside those systems.

Rather than functioning like a traditional conference, CON/FORM is structured as a conversation — a space where experience meets ambition, where legacy meets adaptation, and where honest dialogue can lead to new thinking.

The goal is simple: to create a room where creatives can meet, exchange ideas, recognize shared challenges, and leave with a deeper understanding of how the industry has evolved and where it can go next.


The Meaning of the Name

The name CON/FORM represents a journey.

The first half — CON — represents connection and dialogue.

It unfolds in three stages:

Connect
People meet and discover others working within the same creative ecosystem.

Converse
Conversations begin. Perspectives are exchanged and experiences are shared.

Converge
Through discussion, ideas begin to align and new understanding emerges.

The second half — FORM — represents transformation.

By the end of the experience, participants leave with new perspectives, new relationships, and new ways of thinking about their place in the creative landscape.

The journey of the event therefore follows a simple progression:

Connect → Converse → Converge → Transform


The Structure of the Event

CON/FORM 2026 takes place on March 20th, 2026 at the Afe Babalola Auditorium, University of Lagos.

The event is designed as a single continuous experience that moves through several structured moments during the day.

The program begins with an opening segment that introduces the themes of the gathering and sets the tone for the conversations ahead.

From there, the day unfolds through two central panel conversations.



Panel One — The Architects

This panel features the pioneers and builders of Nigeria’s creative industry.

These are individuals who helped shape the systems that define the industry today. Their work created platforms, institutions, and opportunities that later generations would inherit.

The conversation focuses on legacy, responsibility, and reflection. Speakers discuss the realities of building creative industries in earlier years, what they hoped those systems would become, and what they did not anticipate.

The panel provides the historical context for the conversations that follow.



Panel Two — The Executors

The second panel shifts the perspective to the current generation of creators and performers who are now building careers within those systems.

These are artists, actors, performers, and cultural figures shaping contemporary culture.

They discuss how they navigated the structures that already existed, how they adapted those systems to fit a new generation, and what challenges they encounter while doing so.

This conversation focuses on inheritance, adaptation, and the cost of execution in a rapidly evolving industry.


Systems Interlude

Between these conversations, a short segment highlights the role of institutions and infrastructure that support the creative ecosystem.

Financial institutions, technology platforms, and cultural spaces play an important role in enabling creative work to grow and scale.

This moment explores how those structures help transform creativity into sustainable opportunity.


The Audience

The audience of CON/FORM represents the next layer of the creative ecosystem.

Many attendees move between multiple worlds — corporate careers, creative pursuits, entrepreneurship, and cultural production.

Students, emerging creators, professionals, and industry observers share the same space with established voices.

The audience is not passive. They are part of the conversation and represent the future impact of the ideas discussed in the room.



The Experience

CON/FORM is intentionally designed to feel different from a traditional conference.

The atmosphere is thoughtful rather than formal, and structured but conversational.

Speakers are encouraged to share honest reflections rather than prepared speeches. The emphasis is on truth, insight, and perspective rather than presentation.

The event moves gradually from introduction to dialogue, from dialogue to reflection, and from reflection to new connections between participants.

By the end of the day, the room has shifted — strangers become collaborators, ideas become conversations, and conversations begin forming new possibilities.



Opportunities Around the Event

As part of the event, CON/FORM also invites participation from the wider creative community.

Opportunities are open for:

• Vendors
• Short film submissions
• Sponsors and partners

These contributions allow additional voices and creative expressions to become part of the experience.


The Outcome

CON/FORM is not intended to provide immediate answers to the challenges of the creative industry.

Instead, it creates a space where the right people can meet, speak honestly, and listen to each other.

From those conversations come new relationships, new ideas, and new perspectives that continue long after the event ends.

The intention is simple: bring people together, encourage dialogue, and allow transformation to take shape.
